We travel for the last light

TripDusk is a small, independent travel journal. No sponsored fluff — just real routes, honest costs, and a slight obsession with sunsets.

How it started

One sunset in Lisbon

In 2019, we climbed to a miradouro in Lisbon with takeaway coffee and watched the whole city turn copper. We started keeping a notebook of moments like that — the exact spot, the exact time, what made it special.

That notebook became a website. The website became a community of travelers who plan their trips not around a checklist of sights, but around the hour each place comes alive. That's the whole idea behind TripDusk.

What we believe

Four rules we travel by

Timing is everything

The same view is ordinary at noon and unforgettable at dusk. We plan for the moment, not just the place.

Quality over hype

We'd rather send you to one perfect viewpoint than a list of twenty you'll never reach.

Honest by default

If something isn't worth it, we say so. Our guides are independent and reader-funded.

Travel lightly

Slow routes, local stays, small footprints. We try to leave each place as golden as we found it.
