**The best sunset cruises pair a beautiful stretch of water with the golden hour — Santorini's caldera catamarans, Halong Bay's overnight junks, and Sydney Harbour's tall-ship sails top the list.** They're one of the most reliably romantic things you can book on a trip: a drink in hand, the engine cut, and the sky doing the rest. Here are our favourites and how to book them.

## The cruises worth booking

| Cruise | Where | Why it's special |
|---|---|---|
| **Caldera catamaran** | Santorini, Greece | The world's most famous sunset + swim stops |
| **Overnight junk** | Halong Bay, Vietnam | Karst islands glowing at dusk from your own deck |
| **Tall-ship sail** | Sydney, Australia | Opera House + Harbour Bridge in golden light |
| **Dhow cruise** | Zanzibar, Tanzania | Traditional sail, warm Indian Ocean |
| **Bosphorus cruise** | Istanbul, Turkey | Two continents lit up as the call to prayer sounds |

## How to book a great one

- **Go small.** Private or small-group beats a crowded party boat for romance.
- **Check what's included** — drinks, dinner, hotel pickup — so there are no surprises.
- **Book ahead** in peak season; the best times sell out.
- **Arrive early** to grab the best seats at the bow.
