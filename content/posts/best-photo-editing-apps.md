**For travel and sunset photos, Lightroom mobile is the best all-round editor, Snapseed is the best free one for detailed fixes, and VSCO is best for a consistent film-like look.** Here's what to reach for and when.

## The picks

- **Adobe Lightroom (mobile)** — the standard. Best for RAW, colour, and applying presets for a consistent gallery. Free tier is strong; paid adds cloud sync and more. Pair it with our <a href="tripdusk-preset-pack.html">preset pack</a>.
- **Snapseed (free)** — the best free editor. Brilliant selective edits (brush an area brighter), healing, and curves. Great for quick sunset fixes.
- **VSCO** — beautiful film-style filters and a clean workflow for a cohesive feed look.
- **Photoshop Express / Lightroom presets** — for one-tap looks and quick cleanups.
- **TouchRetouch** — remove distractions (wires, people) from a scene.

## A simple travel workflow

1. Shoot **RAW**.
2. Do the main edit in **Lightroom** (exposure, white balance, HSL) — see our <a href="edit-sunset-photos-lightroom.html">Lightroom tutorial</a>.
3. **Snapseed** for any selective tweak or object removal.
4. Apply a **preset** to keep your look consistent.

<div class="callout"><p><strong>Tip:</strong> pick one editor and learn it well. Jumping between apps for every photo wastes time and breaks your consistent look.</p></div>
