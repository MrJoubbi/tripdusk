## How to book a room that actually faces the sunset

- **Read the room category carefully.** "Sunset view", "caldera view" or "ocean view — west" is what you want. Plain "sea view" can face east.
- **Message the hotel** before paying and ask which direction the room faces. Most reply fast.
- **Higher floors win.** Fewer obstructions, wider horizon.
- **Travel in shoulder season** (late spring / early autumn) for better light and rates.
- **Mention a honeymoon or anniversary** — upgrades and perks are common.
