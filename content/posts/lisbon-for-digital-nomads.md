**Lisbon is one of the best nomad bases in Europe: fast Wi-Fi, a big community, sunshine, an EU visa route, and hills full of sunset viewpoints — for less than Paris or Amsterdam.** Here's how to set up and where to be.

## Where to base yourself

- **Príncipe Real / Santos** — central, leafy, cafés and coworking; a nomad favourite.
- **Alfama** — historic and atmospheric, steep and touristy, great for a shorter stay.
- **Cais do Sodré / Bica** — nightlife and the river close by.
- **Campo de Ourique** — quieter, local, good value.

## Working from Lisbon

- **Coworking** is plentiful — day passes and monthly memberships across the centre.
- **Café culture** is strong; many welcome laptops outside peak hours.
- **Internet** is fast and reliable; confirm a real speed number for any long rental.

## Cost of living

Budget roughly **€1,800–€2,400/month** solo. Central short-term rents are the biggest line; eating out and transport are reasonable.

## The sunsets

Lisbon is a city of **miradouros** (viewpoints): **Senhora do Monte** and **Graça** for the whole city at dusk, **Santa Catarina** over the river. Grab a drink and join the locals.
