**The best sunset dinners pair a great table with the golden hour — clifftops, rooftops and beach tables where the view is the main course.** Here are 20 worth planning a night around, and how to book the right seat.

## A world tour of sunset tables

- **Santorini (Oia)** — caldera-edge terraces; the benchmark.
- **Positano & Ravello, Italy** — clifftop tables over the Amalfi Coast.
- **Uluwatu, Bali** — dinner above the Indian Ocean.
- **Mykonos & Naxos, Greece** — waterline tables and "Little Venice" sunsets.
- **Santa Teresa, Costa Rica** — beach tables, bare feet in the sand.
- **Cape Town, South Africa** — Camps Bay strip facing the Atlantic.
- **Dubrovnik, Croatia** — cliff bars built into the old city walls.
- **Key West, USA** — Gulf-front tables and the nightly sunset ritual.

…plus rooftop restaurants in **Bangkok, Lisbon, Marrakech, Mexico City, Istanbul** and more — most cities have one great sunset table if you know to ask.

## How to book the right table

- **Reserve early** and request an **outdoor/window table with the sunset view** — say it explicitly.
- **Book a seating ~45 minutes before sunset.**
- **Check which way the terrace faces** before you commit.
- **Order a drink first**, watch the light, then eat into the blue hour.
