**You don't need a five-star resort for romance — small boutique and family-run hotels across Portugal, Greece, Mexico, Southeast Asia and Eastern Europe deliver character, rooftops and sea views for under $150 a night.** Here's how to find them.

## Where $150 goes far

- **Portugal & Greece** (shoulder season) — whitewashed guesthouses with terraces.
- **Mexico (Oaxaca, Mérida)** — colourful courtyard hotels with rooftop bars.
- **Southeast Asia (Bali, Vietnam, Thailand)** — boutique stays with pools well under budget.
- **Eastern Europe (Kotor, Ljubljana, Porto)** — character and views for less.

## How to spot a gem

- **Look for a rooftop or terrace** for your own sunset spot.
- **Book a room with a view** — and message to confirm which way it faces.
- **Read recent reviews** for the words "charming", "quiet", and "view".
- **Stay central** so you can walk to dinner.
- **Travel shoulder season** — the same room can be half the summer price.

<div class="callout"><p><strong>Tip:</strong> family-run places often upgrade honeymooners or anniversaries for free — just mention it when you book.</p></div>
