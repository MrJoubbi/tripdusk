**Nomad visas are easiest in Georgia and parts of the Caribbean (fast, low-friction, often online), while Portugal, Spain, Croatia and Estonia offer the most established European routes.** Here's a rough ranking by how painless the process is — always confirm current rules before applying.

## Easiest and fastest

- **Georgia** — long visa-free stays for many nationalities, plus a simple remote-work program. Very low friction.
- **Caribbean (Barbados, Antigua, etc.)** — straightforward, mostly online applications for a 1–2 year stay.
- **Estonia** — the original, well-run digital nomad visa with clear guidance.

## Popular and well-documented

- **Portugal** — a strong nomad/residence route; higher income threshold but a great base.
- **Spain** — a solid nomad visa with a growing community; some bureaucracy.
- **Croatia** — a simple, popular option for a year on the Adriatic.

## Worth checking

- **Thailand, Indonesia (Bali), Colombia, Brazil, UAE** — all now offer remote-work or nomad visas with varying requirements.

## What you'll usually need

- **Proof of remote income** (a monthly minimum).
- **Health insurance** — see <a href="best-travel-insurance-digital-nomads.html">nomad insurance</a>.
- **A clean background check** and a valid passport.
- **Accommodation** proof, sometimes.

<div class="callout"><p><strong>Tip:</strong> requirements and income thresholds change often — check the official government page (not a blog) right before you apply.</p></div>
