**Travel insurance covers five main things: emergency medical and hospital care, emergency evacuation and repatriation, trip cancellation or interruption, lost or damaged baggage and gear, and personal liability — but the details and exclusions vary a lot between policies.** Here's what each one means.

## The pillars, explained

- **Emergency medical & hospital** — treatment if you get sick or injured abroad. The most important cover; aim for a high limit ($250k+).
- **Evacuation & repatriation** — getting you to proper care, or home, in a serious emergency. This can cost tens of thousands on its own.
- **Trip cancellation / interruption** — refunds if you have to cancel or cut a trip short for a covered reason. Common in holiday policies, less so in nomad ones.
- **Baggage & gear** — replaces lost, stolen or damaged luggage. Electronics are often capped low — check the limit for your laptop and camera.
- **Personal liability** — if you accidentally injure someone or damage property.

## The exclusions that catch people out

- **Pre-existing conditions** not declared.
- **Adventure activities** (diving, motorbikes, trekking at altitude) unless added.
- **Alcohol/drug-related** incidents.
- **Valuables left unattended** or not in a locked place.
- **Regions under government travel warnings.**
