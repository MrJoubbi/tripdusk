**A perfect Amalfi Coast honeymoon splits ten days between Positano and Ravello, with day trips to Capri and a slow finish on the water — timing dinners for the moment the coast turns gold.** Here's the itinerary we'd book again.

## The 10-day plan

- **Days 1–4 — Positano.** Settle into the cliffside town, beach mornings, boat trip to the fishing village of Nerano for lunch, and sunset from a clifftop terrace.
- **Days 5–7 — Ravello.** Higher, quieter, greener. The Villa Rufolo and Villa Cimbrone gardens have the best views on the whole coast.
- **Days 8–9 — Capri (day or overnight).** The Blue Grotto, a chairlift up Monte Solaro, and an aperitivo in the Piazzetta.
- **Day 10 — Slow finish.** A private boat afternoon along the coast before you fly home.
