**The best rooftop sunsets are in cities built for terrace culture — Bangkok's sky bars, Lisbon's miradouros, Mexico City and Marrakech's rooftops — where you get the skyline and the golden hour in one drink.** Here's a city-by-city starting list.

## City by city

- **Bangkok** — the original sky-bar city; dozens of high-rise terraces over the river.
- **Lisbon** — rooftop bars plus the free **miradouros** (viewpoints) at Graça and Senhora do Monte.
- **Mexico City** — Roma and Centro rooftops looking to the mountains.
- **Marrakech** — medina terraces over the rooftops as the call to prayer sounds.
- **Athens** — rooftops framing the floodlit Acropolis at dusk.
- **New York** — skyline rooftops in Manhattan and Brooklyn.
- **Barcelona** — hotel rooftops over the city and sea.
- **Singapore** — sky bars with skyline-and-harbour views.

## How to do it right

- **Arrive 45–60 minutes early**, especially on weekends — the good tables go fast.
- **Check if it takes reservations** and book if so.
- **Know which way it faces** — you want west for the sun, or east for the skyline lighting up.
- **One drink for the sunset, dinner after** at street level is a great combo.

<div class="callout"><p><strong>Tip:</strong> free public viewpoints (hills, bridges, waterfronts) often beat paid rooftops — check both.</p></div>
