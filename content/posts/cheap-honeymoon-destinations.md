**You don't need $10,000 for a dream honeymoon — Zanzibar, Bali, Mexico's Riviera Maya, Portugal and the smaller Greek islands all deliver sunsets, romance and warm water for two under $3,000, especially in shoulder season.** Here's where the money goes furthest.

## Best value honeymoon destinations

- **Zanzibar, Tanzania** — wide beaches and dhow-boat sunsets at a fraction of the Maldives' price. Best June–October.
- **Bali, Indonesia** — clifftop villas, temples and beach clubs; huge range of romantic stays for the money.
- **Riviera Maya, Mexico** — cenotes, ruins and Caribbean beaches, easy to reach from North America.
- **Portugal (Algarve or Madeira)** — golden cliffs and great food without a long-haul price tag.
- **Naxos or Milos, Greece** — the Cyclades romance of Santorini for noticeably less.

## How to keep it under $3k

- **Shoulder season** — late spring or early autumn: warm light, thin crowds, lower rates.
- **Fly midweek** and book flights early.
- **Pick one splurge** — a private sunset cruise or one night in a special stay — and keep the rest mid-range.
- **Mention it's your honeymoon** for free upgrades and perks.
