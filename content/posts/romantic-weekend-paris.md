**For a romantic weekend in Paris, base yourself centrally, walk everywhere, and plan each evening around the light — sunset from Sacré-Cœur on the first night, the Eiffel Tower sparkling from Trocadéro on the second.** Here's a relaxed 48-hour itinerary built for couples, with the sunset moments that make it unforgettable.

## Day 1 — Montmartre & the golden hour

- **Morning:** coffee and croissants in the Marais, then wander its lanes and Place des Vosges.
- **Afternoon:** the Musée d'Orsay (smaller and more romantic than the Louvre), then a slow walk along the Seine.
- **Sunset:** climb to **Sacré-Cœur** in Montmartre. The steps face west over the whole city — arrive 45 minutes early, bring wine, and watch Paris turn gold.
- **Dinner:** a cand-lit bistro in Montmartre's back streets.

## Day 2 — Islands, gardens & the sparkle

- **Morning:** Île de la Cité and Île Saint-Louis — get a Berthillon ice cream and cross the little bridges.
- **Afternoon:** the Luxembourg Gardens for a lazy hour, then browse Saint-Germain.
- **Sunset:** the **Trocadéro** terrace for the classic Eiffel Tower view as the sky fades — then stay for the tower's hourly sparkle after dark.
- **Dinner:** a Seine-side table, or a river cruise dinner if you want to splurge.
