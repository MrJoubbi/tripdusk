**When we had a medical emergency abroad, the claim was paid in under three weeks — because we called the insurer first and kept every single document.** Here's exactly how it went, so you know what to do if it happens to you.

## What happened

A kitchen accident on a long trip meant an ER visit, stitches, and a follow-up. Not dramatic, but not cheap either — the kind of thing that's genuinely common on the road.

## The steps that worked

1. **We called the insurer's 24/7 line first.** They confirmed the hospital was covered and told us what to keep.
2. **We kept everything** — itemised bills, the medical report, prescriptions, receipts, and photos of it all on our phones.
3. **We paid up front and claimed back** (common for smaller amounts) — the insurer reimbursed to our bank.
4. **We filed within the deadline** through the online portal, uploading the documents.
5. **We answered their follow-up question fast** — one missing detail is what slows most claims.

Payment landed in about **18 days**.

## What we'd tell anyone

- **Call before you pay** for anything major — some things must be pre-authorised.
- **Document obsessively.** No receipt, no payout.
- **Know your deadline** — claims have time limits.
- **Buy enough cover.** A high medical limit is the whole point. See <a href="what-travel-insurance-covers.html">what travel insurance actually covers</a>.
