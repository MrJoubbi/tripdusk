**You can predict a great sunset with the right apps — they score the likely quality from cloud height, coverage and air clarity, so you know the night before whether it's worth setting an alarm.** Here's what to use and what to look for.

## The apps to use

- **A dedicated sunset-quality forecast** — apps that give a percentage or rating for the evening's sunset based on cloud and atmosphere data.
- **A golden-hour / sun-position app** — shows exact sunset time and where on the horizon the sun will set, so you pick the right viewpoint.
- **A reliable weather app with cloud layers** — check **cloud height and coverage**, not just "sunny/cloudy".

## What actually makes a great sunset

- **Mid- and high-level clouds** — something for the colour to paint. A totally clear sky is often flat.
- **Clear air near the horizon** — so the sun's light reaches those clouds. Total overcast kills it.
- **A little humidity or haze** can deepen the colours.

## The routine

1. **Check the forecast the night before** and again a couple of hours prior.
2. **Use the sun-position app** to pick a west-facing spot with a clean horizon.
3. **Arrive 30 minutes early** and stay for the blue hour — see <a href="golden-hour-vs-blue-hour.html">golden hour vs blue hour</a>.
