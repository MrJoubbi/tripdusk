## What travel insurance actually covers

Policies vary, but the pillars are:

- **Emergency medical & hospital** — the big one; make sure the limit is high (aim for $250k+).
- **Emergency evacuation & repatriation** — getting you to proper care, or home, in a crisis.
- **Trip interruption / cancellation** — some nomad policies include this, many don't.
- **Baggage & gear** — laptops and cameras are often capped low; check the electronics limit.
- **Personal liability** — if you accidentally injure someone or damage property.

## How to choose the right policy

- **Match it to your trip length.** Open-ended? A subscription (SafetyWing, Genki) beats buying repeated short policies.
- **Check the medical limit** and whether your destinations (especially the US) are included — US cover costs more.
- **Look at the deductible** (excess) — a lower premium often means a higher out-of-pocket cost per claim.
- **Confirm gear cover** if you travel with expensive electronics.
- **Read the activity exclusions** if you do anything adventurous.
- **Buy before you need it** — you can't claim for something that started before cover began.
