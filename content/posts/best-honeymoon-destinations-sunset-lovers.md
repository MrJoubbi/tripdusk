**For couples who plan the day around the light, the best sunset honeymoons are Santorini (Greece), the Maldives, Bora Bora, the Amalfi Coast (Italy), and Zanzibar (Tanzania).** Each one turns the end of the day into the main event — a caldera glowing pink, an overwater deck facing an empty horizon, a clifftop dinner as the sea goes gold. Below are our favourites, when to go, where to stay for the view, and how to book without overpaying.

## The best sunset honeymoon destinations at a glance

| Destination | Best for | Sunset highlight | Best months |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Santorini, Greece** | The classic | Oia caldera glow over the sea | May–Jun, Sep |
| **Maldives** | Total privacy | Overwater deck, empty horizon | Nov–Apr |
| **Bora Bora** | Once-in-a-lifetime | Mount Otemanu at golden hour | May–Oct |
| **Amalfi Coast, Italy** | Food + romance | Clifftop dinner as the sea turns gold | May–Jun, Sep |
| **Zanzibar, Tanzania** | Value + beaches | Dhow boats silhouetted at dusk | Jun–Oct |

### Santorini, Greece — the classic for a reason

Oia's sunset is the benchmark every other is measured against: whitewashed villages tumbling down a caldera that glows amber and rose as the sun drops into the Aegean. It gets busy, so the move is to book a room or terrace *with* a caldera view and skip the crowd entirely. ### The Maldives — sunsets with total privacy

If you want the horizon to yourselves, an overwater villa in the Maldives delivers. The best resorts orient villas **west** for sunset and east for sunrise — check which before you book. Dry season (**November–April**) means calm, clear evenings. It's a splurge, but honeymoon and stay-longer offers cut the cost meaningfully. <a href="#" rel="sponsored nofollow">Compare overwater resorts →</a>

### Bora Bora — the once-in-a-lifetime one

Mount Otemanu rising over a turquoise lagoon, lit up at golden hour, is hard to beat. It pairs beautifully as a two-stop trip with a few nights on Tahiti or Moorea. Peak season (**May–October**) is dry and bright. <a href="#" rel="sponsored nofollow">See Bora Bora resorts →</a>

### Amalfi Coast, Italy — for food-loving couples

Positano and Ravello give you clifftop dinners where the whole coastline turns gold. It's romance with substance — lemon terraces, long lunches, and boat trips to hidden coves. Come in **late spring or September**. <a href="#" rel="sponsored nofollow">Browse Amalfi hotels →</a>

### Zanzibar, Tanzania — beauty on a smaller budget

For couples who want the tropics without the tropical price tag, Zanzibar's west coast (Kendwa, Nungwi) serves up wide beaches and dhow boats silhouetted against a burning sky. Ideal add-on after a safari. Best in the **dry season, June–October**.

## How to book a sunset honeymoon without overpaying

- **Book the room for the view, not just the resort.** A "sunset-facing" or "caldera-view" category is worth the upgrade on a honeymoon.
- **Travel in shoulder season.** Late spring and early autumn give you the best light, thinner crowds, and lower rates.
- **Say it's your honeymoon.** Many hotels add perks — a room upgrade, sparkling wine, a late checkout — if you mention it when booking.
- **Lock in flights + first nights early.** Overwater villas and caldera rooms sell out 4–6 months ahead in peak season.
