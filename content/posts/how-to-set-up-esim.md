**To set up an eSIM: check your phone is compatible and unlocked, buy a plan to get a QR code, add the eSIM in Settings by scanning it, set it as your data line, and turn on Data Roaming for that line only.** It takes about five minutes. Here's the full walk-through.

## Step by step

1. **Check compatibility.** Your phone must be eSIM-capable and carrier-unlocked. Most phones since 2019 qualify.
2. **Buy a plan.** Choose a country or regional plan in a provider's app — see our <a href="best-esims-long-term-travel.html">best eSIMs guide</a>. You'll get a QR code.
3. **Add the eSIM.** iPhone: `Settings → Cellular → Add eSIM`. Android: `Settings → Network & internet → SIMs → Add eSIM`. Scan the QR code.
4. **Set your data line** to the new eSIM; keep your home SIM for calls and texts.
5. **Turn on Data Roaming for the eSIM line only.** Required abroad — and it won't trigger home-carrier charges.

<div class="callout"><p><strong>Tip:</strong> install the eSIM while you still have Wi-Fi (you need a connection to activate it), but start the plan when you land so the validity window doesn't tick down early.</p></div>

## Mistakes to avoid

- Leaving **Data Roaming off** on the eSIM line (the #1 issue).
- Setting the **wrong line for data**.
- **Deleting** the eSIM by accident — it can't always be reinstalled from the same QR.
