**For most travellers, an eSIM is the best default — instant, app-based, and easy across borders — while a local SIM wins for a long stay in one country, and pocket Wi-Fi only makes sense for groups.** Here's the honest comparison.

## The three options

| Option | Best for | Downsides |
|---|---|---|
| **eSIM** | Arrivals, short stays, border-hopping | Slightly higher per-GB than local |
| **Local SIM** | Long stays in one country | Shop visit, ID rules, swap needed |
| **Pocket Wi-Fi** | Groups sharing one connection | Extra device to charge/return, rental cost |

### eSIM
Online the moment you land, no shop, keep your home number, switch countries from the app. The easy choice for most trips. See our <a href="best-esims-long-term-travel.html">best eSIMs guide</a>.

### Local SIM
Usually the cheapest **per GB** if you're staying put in one country for weeks. The catch: finding a shop, possible passport registration, and swapping cards.

### Pocket Wi-Fi
A rented hotspot device. Useful for a **group** sharing one connection, or an old phone with no eSIM. Otherwise it's another thing to charge, carry and return.

## The practical answer

- **Solo or couple, multi-country:** eSIM.
- **One country, a month or more:** eSIM for arrival, then a local SIM.
- **Family or group:** consider pocket Wi-Fi, or one eSIM hotspotting to the rest.

<div class="callout"><p><strong>Tip:</strong> even on a local SIM, keep a small eSIM plan as backup for the day the local network fails.</p></div>
