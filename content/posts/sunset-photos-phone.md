**To shoot a pro sunset on your phone: tap the sky to lock exposure, drag down to keep the colours rich, shoot RAW, skip digital zoom, keep steady, and edit lightly.** The camera you have beats the one you left at home — here's how to get the most from it.

## The technique

- **Tap the sky, then drag down.** Tap to set focus/exposure on the bright part of the sky, then slide the exposure down a touch so colours stay saturated instead of blown out.
- **Turn on RAW / ProRAW** if your phone has it — far more to work with when editing.
- **Skip digital zoom.** It just crops and softens. Walk closer or use the true optical lens.
- **Try HDR off.** Sometimes the "worse" exposure is the more dramatic photo.
- **Steady it.** Brace on a wall or use a mini tripod as the light drops.

## Composition that works

- **Add a foreground silhouette** — a person, tree, or building for depth.
- **Use the rule of thirds** — put the horizon on the lower or upper third, not dead centre.
- **Shoot wide and tight** — a sweeping scene and a detail of the sun through something.

## Edit lightly

Nudge warmth up, lift shadows, add a little vibrance, pull highlights down. Our <a href="edit-sunset-photos-lightroom.html">free Lightroom tutorial</a> works on the mobile app too.

<div class="callout"><p><strong>Tip:</strong> stay for the blue hour — the 20 minutes after the sun sets often give phones their richest colours.</p></div>
