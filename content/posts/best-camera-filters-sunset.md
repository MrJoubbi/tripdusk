**The filters that make sunsets pop are the graduated ND (to balance a bright sky against a dark foreground), the polariser (to deepen colour and cut glare), and the standard ND (for long exposures). You don't need expensive ones to see a difference.**

## The three that matter

- **Graduated ND (GND)** — dark at the top, clear at the bottom. Slide it so the dark part covers the bright sky, balancing the exposure in one frame. The most useful sunset filter.
- **Polariser (CPL)** — deepens blue sky, cuts glare and reflections on water, and boosts saturation. Rotate to taste.
- **Neutral density (ND)** — cuts light for long exposures — silky water and moving clouds at dusk. Needs a tripod.

## Buying tips

- **Match the thread size** to your lens (check the number after the ⌀ symbol).
- **Screw-in kits** are cheapest; **square filter systems** are more flexible if you go deeper.
