**A good travel tripod under $80 keeps your sunset and blue-hour shots sharp without weighing down your bag — look for low weight, a small folded size, and a load rating above your camera.** Here's what to buy and why.

## What to look for

- **Weight & folded size** — it has to actually fit in your bag or you won't carry it.
- **Load rating** — must exceed your camera + lens weight for stability.
- **Leg locks** — flip locks are fast; twist locks are compact. Either is fine.
- **A usable head** — a ball head lets you compose quickly.
- **Phone mount** — get an adapter so one tripod works for camera and phone.

## The types (all under $80)

- **Compact travel tripod** — full height, folds small; the best all-rounder.
- **Tabletop / mini tripod** — tiny and light; rest it on a wall or rock.
- **Flexible (bendy-leg) tripod** — wraps around railings and branches; great for phones. <a href="#" rel="sponsored nofollow">See travel tripods →</a>
