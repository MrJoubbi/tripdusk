**The most romantic seaside towns are the walkable ones with good coffee, an easy pace, and a coastline made for sunset strolls.** These seven are built for couples who want to do very little, beautifully.

## The seven

1. **Cassis, France** — pastel harbour and dramatic calanques next door.
2. **Nazaré or Tavira, Portugal** — Algarve light, fresh seafood, quiet beaches.
3. **Cadaqués, Spain** — whitewashed Costa Brava village Dalí loved.
4. **Vernazza, Italy** — the prettiest of the Cinque Terre for sunset.
5. **Kotor Bay towns, Montenegro** — fjord-like scenery, fewer crowds.
6. **Hydra, Greece** — no cars, donkeys, and golden stone at dusk.
7. **Sidmouth or St Ives, England** — for a cosy, closer-to-home escape.

## How to do it right

- **Stay central** so you can walk everywhere.
- **Build the day around two meals and a sunset walk** — that's the whole plan.
- **Go shoulder season** for warm, quiet, golden light.

<div class="callout"><p><strong>Tip:</strong> pick one town and stay put. Slow travel is the point — resist the urge to town-hop.</p></div>
