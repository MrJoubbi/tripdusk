**The nomads who stay productive don't rely on willpower — they build a simple routine: a fixed deep-work block, calls batched into one window, separate spaces for work and rest, and slow travel so the routine survives.** Here's the system.

## The routine that works

- **Protect a deep-work block.** Two to four uninterrupted hours at your best time of day, every day. Guard it.
- **Batch your calls.** Pick one window that overlaps your team's time zone and put every meeting there — leaving the rest free for focus.
- **Separate work and rest.** Even a different chair or café for work vs. relaxing tells your brain which mode you're in.
- **Travel slowly.** A month per city keeps your time zone, Wi-Fi and habits stable — the enemy of productivity is constant packing.
- **End with the sunset.** Close the laptop and go watch the light. A hard stop makes the work hours sharper.

## Handling time zones

- **Base yourself for overlap** — Latin America for US teams, Europe/Africa for European teams, SE Asia for Australia.
- **Agree a fixed meeting window** with your team and defend the rest.
- **Do async well** — clear written updates mean fewer calls.
