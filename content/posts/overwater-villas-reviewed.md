**Overwater villas are worth it for a honeymoon or milestone trip — the privacy and the step-off-your-deck water access are the real magic — but not every villa lives up to the brochure.** Here's what actually matters when you book one.

## What the photos don't tell you

- **Not all villas face the sunset.** Resorts split them into sunrise and sunset rows — always ask for **sunset-facing**.
- **Water quality varies villa to villa.** Ones over clear lagoon beat ones over sand or seagrass. Ask for a villa over clear water.
- **Privacy depends on spacing.** Some resorts pack villas tightly. Check photos and reviews for how close the neighbours are.
- **Reef distance matters** if you want to snorkel from your deck.

## Where they're worth it

- **Maldives** — the widest choice and the best-value honeymoon deals.
- **Bora Bora** — the iconic Mount Otemanu backdrop.
- **Zanzibar & the Caribbean** — a handful of options, often cheaper.
