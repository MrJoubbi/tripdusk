**Golden hour is the ~30 minutes before sunset when light is warm and directional; blue hour is the ~20–30 minutes after the sun sets when the sky turns deep blue and pink. Shoot through both — most people leave right when it's getting good.**

## Golden hour

- **When:** roughly the last 30 minutes before sunset (and first 30 after sunrise).
- **Light:** warm, soft, low and directional — flattering and full of long shadows.
- **Best for:** silhouettes, warm portraits, landscapes with texture.
- **Settings:** low ISO, f/8–f/11, warm white balance, expose for the sky.

## Blue hour

- **When:** the 20–30 minutes *after* the sun dips below the horizon.
- **Light:** even and cool, with deep blues and lingering pinks; city lights start to glow.
- **Best for:** moody skies, cityscapes, water reflections, rich colour.
- **Settings:** you'll need a tripod as shutter speeds slow right down.

## The takeaway

Arrive **30 minutes before** sunset and stay **30 minutes after**. That one habit — not leaving when the sun disappears — is what separates good sunset photos from great ones.
