**The best sunset spots in the world span every kind of landscape — Oia in Santorini for the sea, Uluru in Australia for the desert, Bagan in Myanmar for temples, and Big Sur in California for wild coastline.** Below is our running list of 25, grouped by continent, with when to go and when to arrive so you don't miss the light.

<div class="callout"><p><strong>Tip:</strong> the golden rule for all of these — arrive 30 minutes early, and don't leave when the sun disappears. The blue hour just after sunset is often the best part. Here's <a href="golden-hour.html#tips">how to photograph it</a>.</p></div>

## Europe

- **Oia, Santorini (Greece)** — the benchmark caldera sunset. Arrive very early; it's popular.
- **Cap de Formentor, Mallorca (Spain)** — a lighthouse on a dramatic headland.
- **Ponta da Piedade, Lagos (Portugal)** — golden cliffs and sea caves on the Algarve.
- **Sintra & Cabo da Roca (Portugal)** — mainland Europe's westernmost sunset.
- **Santa Maria del Fiore rooftops, Florence (Italy)** — the city turns terracotta.

## Africa & Middle East

- **Serengeti / Maasai Mara** — acacia silhouettes and huge skies; peak safari magic.
- **Zanzibar west coast (Tanzania)** — dhow boats against a burning horizon.
- **Sahara dunes, Merzouga (Morocco)** — the desert shifts through gold and violet.
- **Cape Town, Signal Hill (South Africa)** — the city, the sea, and Table Mountain.
- **Wadi Rum (Jordan)** — Mars-red desert at dusk.

## Asia

- **Bagan (Myanmar)** — thousands of temple silhouettes under a hazy amber sky.
- **Uluwatu, Bali (Indonesia)** — clifftop temple over the Indian Ocean.
- **Mount Batur, Bali (Indonesia)** — a sunrise-and-sunset volcano hike.
- **Angkor Wat (Cambodia)** — ancient towers reflected in the moat.
- **Li River, Guilin (China)** — karst peaks fading into mist.

## The Americas

- **Grand Canyon, Hopi Point (USA)** — the canyon walls blaze red-orange.
- **Big Sur, Highway 1 (USA)** — Pacific fog and golden cliffs.
- **Key West (USA)** — the nightly Mallory Square sunset celebration.
- **Uyuni Salt Flats (Bolivia)** — a mirror sky at the edge of the wet season.
- **Rio de Janeiro, Arpoador (Brazil)** — locals applaud as the sun drops behind the mountains.

## Oceania

- **Uluru (Australia)** — the rock glows through ochre, rust and violet.
- **Cape Naturaliste (Australia)** — wildflowers and the Indian Ocean.
- **Piha Beach (New Zealand)** — black sand and wild surf.
- **Bora Bora (French Polynesia)** — Mount Otemanu over a turquoise lagoon.
- **Cable Beach, Broome (Australia)** — camel trains silhouetted on the sand.

## How to catch a great one anywhere

- **Check the sunset time and direction** the night before — a free golden-hour app does both.
- **Arrive 30 minutes early** to claim your spot and read the scene.
- **Stay for blue hour** — the 20–30 minutes after sunset often beat the sunset itself.
- **Have a foreground** — a person, a tree, a temple — to give the shot depth.
